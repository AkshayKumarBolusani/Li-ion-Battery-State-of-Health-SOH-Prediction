🔬 KEY TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚗️  ICA (Incremental Capacity Analysis)
   └─ dQ/dV computation with Savitzky-Golay smoothing
   └─ Peak detection for degradation tracking
   └─ Peak shifts: ~0.15V over battery lifetime

🧠 CNN-LSTM Architecture
   └─ CNN: Extract spatial patterns (32→64→64 filters)
   └─ LSTM: Capture temporal dynamics (2 layers, 128 hidden)
   └─ FC: Regression head (64→32→1)
   └─ Total params: ~200K

📈 Physics-Guided Features
   └─ Not just capacity fade
   └─ ICA peaks track electrode phase transitions
   └─ DCIR indicates resistance increase
   └─ Temperature stability monitoring

⏰ Time-Based Validation
   └─ Train: Early degradation (cycles 0-70%)
   └─ Test: Late degradation (cycles 70-100%)
   └─ No shuffling (respects chronology)
